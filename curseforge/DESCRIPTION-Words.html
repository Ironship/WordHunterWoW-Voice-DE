Every word in the German dictionary, spoken. Click a word in a quest and hear it — for people learning German by playing World of Warcraft in German.

104,274 clips, 28.8 hours. One for every entry in the German dictionary; each has been checked against its word by a speech recogniser and re-read where it did not match.

Needs QuestWordHunter

QuestWordHunter is required, and this pack is mainly for it: a word is spoken when you click it, and clicking a word is what its quest panel offers. Without that addon these clips are files nothing can reach. The reader itself is inside this pack, so nothing else is needed — if you still have the separate German Voiceover engine addon from before, remove it.

What this pack does, and what it does not

It speaks single words, one at a time, when you click one. It speaks no quest text at all — not for any expansion. Quest narration is in the other packs, and this one is used together with them.

The four packs, and what each one speaks

  • QuestWordHunter — German Voiceover: Classic — Classic, Burning Crusade and Wrath
  • QuestWordHunter — German Voiceover: Cataclysm — Cataclysm, Pandaria, Draenor and Legion
  • QuestWordHunter — German Voiceover: Modern — Battle for Azeroth, Shadowlands, Dragonflight and The War Within
  • QuestWordHunter — German Voiceover: Words — every word in the German dictionary, spoken when you click one — needs QuestWordHunter — this page

Install as many as you play; they do not overlap, and no clip is in two of them. Each carries the same reader, and one reader runs however many packs are installed. CurseForge allows 2 GB per file and all of the audio is three times that, which is why it cannot be one download.

Settings

/whwv, or Options → AddOns → QuestWordHunter Voice: quests on or off, single words, the window that shows who is speaking, a delay before the reading starts, the pause between sentences. The page lists the packs you have installed and says which pack's copy of the reader is running.

How it was made

Read from Blizzard's German quest text by a neural text-to-speech model, one clip per sentence, and checked with a speech recogniser.
