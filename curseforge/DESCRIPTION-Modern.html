The German a quest giver says, spoken aloud as the quest window opens — for Battle for Azeroth, Shadowlands, Dragonflight and The War Within. For people learning German by playing World of Warcraft in German.

87,262 clips, 97.0 hours — one clip per sentence across the offer, the progress line and the hand-in, quest ids 48,159 and up.

Nothing else to install

The reader is inside this pack. Install it, open a quest, and it reads. Play several expansions? Install the pack for each — the reader runs once however many are installed. If you still have the separate German Voiceover engine addon from before, remove it.

With QuestWordHunter

QuestWordHunter is optional. With it, every paragraph gets a play button, the sentence being read lights up as it is spoken, and a clicked word is said out loud — that last one needs the Words pack.

The other packs

CurseForge allows 2 GB per file, and all of the audio is three times that, so it comes as four packs. No clip is in two of them; take the ones for what you play.

  • QuestWordHunter — German Voiceover: Classic — Classic, Burning Crusade and Wrath
  • QuestWordHunter — German Voiceover: Cataclysm — Cataclysm, Pandaria, Draenor and Legion
  • QuestWordHunter — German Voiceover: Words — the spoken dictionary, for clicking single words

Settings

/whwv, or Options → AddOns → QuestWordHunter Voice: quests on or off, single words, the window that shows who is speaking, a delay before the reading starts, the pause between sentences. The page lists the packs you have installed and says which pack's copy of the reader is running.

How it was made

Read from Blizzard's German quest text by a neural text-to-speech model, one clip per sentence, and checked with a speech recogniser.
